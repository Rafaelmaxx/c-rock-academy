Bring Indoor Climbing
to Charlottesville

We're exploring the idea of bringing a new indoor climbing gym to the Charlottesville area-built around rope climbing, community, and a love for the outdoors.

Our goal is to create a welcoming space where experienced climbers can challenge themselves, beginners can learn, and people can connect through climbing.

Our Vision

More Than a Climbing Gym - A Local Climbing Community Climbing Community tunities for recreation, training, learning, and most of all, connection.

Potential offerings may include:

Top-rope climbing and, potentially, lead climbing.

Core Rope Terrain

Auto-belays and beginner-friendly climbing experiences.

Accessible Solo & Intro

Climbing instruction and skills development.

Clinics & Education

Youth programs, group events, and community gatherings.

Families & Groups

A welcoming social space for climbers to connect.

Community Hub

Potential in house cafe

Coffee & Post-Climb Hangout

These features are ideas under consideration and will depend on feasibility, community interest, and the final facility.

Our Mission

Why We're Exploring This

Climbing is about more than reaching the top. It's about problem-solving, confidence, movement, and community.

We want to explore whether Charlottesville and the surrounding communities would support another indoor climbing option, particularly one focused on rope climbing and a welcoming experience for climbers at different stages of their journey.

Before committing to a facility, we want to hear directly from the people who might use it.

Meet the Owners

Meet the Founders

A shared love of climbing, adventure, and bringing people together.

We're Marina and Dennis, and our shared love of climbing, adventure, and the outdoors inspired us to imagine C-Rock.

Marina's love of climbing began at just three years old, climbing alongside her dad on his own climbing wall in Brazil. What started as a childhood experience grew into a lifelong appreciation for adventure, the outdoors, and trying new things. Dennis is an experienced climber with a deep passion for the sport. He loves the physical challenge, the technique and problem-solving that go into every climb, and the opportunity to keep learning and pushing his limits.

Over the years, we've explored new places and cultures, including climbing in Brazil and Spain. Eventually, our journey brought us to Charlottesville, a place we quickly grew to love and knew we wanted to call home. Some of our favorite memories together have come from discovering new climbing spots, exploring the outdoors, and sharing new experiences along the way.

Those experiences helped shape the idea behind C-Rock. We envision a welcoming place where people can challenge themselves, learn something new, and celebrate every little victory. Whether you're an experienced climber, trying the sport for the first time, or showing up on your own, we want you to feel comfortable, encouraged, and part of something.

For us, C-Rock is about more than climbing. It's about creating a community where people can meet, grow stronger, build confidence, try something new, and share a little adventure along the way.

We're excited to explore this idea with the Charlottesville community and invite others to be part of the journey from the very beginning.
